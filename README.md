## Rocket League 3D

A complete, single-page 3D car-soccer game built with Three.js. Drive, boost, jump, flip, and fly through a neon night arena against 2v2 or 1v1 AI.

### Play

```bash
./start.sh
```

Then open the local URL printed by the dev server.

### Controls

- `W A S D` or arrow keys — drive, steer, air pitch/yaw
- `Space` — jump; press again in the air to double-jump / flip
- `Shift` — boost
- `Q` / `E` — air roll
- `C` — toggle ball camera
- `Esc` / `P` — pause

Gamepad and multi-touch controls are supported. On phones and tablets, drag the left stick to drive and use the JUMP and BOOST buttons.
