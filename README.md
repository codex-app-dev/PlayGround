## BIONEURAL: The Synapse Run

A complete, browser-playable neon platform game. Recover all three memory shards in the Synapse Foundry and reach the uplink before losing your signal.

### Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve this folder with any static web server. Use **A/D** or **←/→** to move, **Space** or **↑** to jump, and **Q** to switch between Material and Echo planes. On touch screens, use the on-screen controls. Press **R** to reboot the run.

The Material and Echo planes have different suspended bridges. The main floor is shared; switch planes in midair to route across gaps and collect every shard. Checkpoints preserve your progress through the level.
