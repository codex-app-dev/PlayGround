    How Swap Chess works

    takes
    gains
    N
    1. 1
      Every capture is a trade.

      When you take a piece, your piece keeps its shape but adopts the captured piece's movement power. The captured piece leaves with your old power.

    2. 2
      Watch the badges.

      A small badge appears whenever a piece's power differs from its shape. A rook with an N badge moves like a knight; a pawn with a Q badge moves like a queen.

    3. 3
      Pawn power promotes.

      Any piece carrying pawn power that reaches the far rank becomes queen power. This can happen after a capture, too.

    4. 4
      The crown is still the crown.

      Kings keep their identity. Checkmate wins. There is no castling or en passant, and pawns may only double-step from their home rank.
