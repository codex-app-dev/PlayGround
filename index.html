THE TWIST

Power travels.

Power Shift is chess with a living board. Pieces keep their shape, but not their movement.

01

Capture to swap

When your piece captures, it inherits the captured piece's movement power. The captured piece leaves the board.

♞ × ♜ → R
02

Read the badge

A small badge appears whenever a piece's movement differs from its shape. A pawn with a queen badge moves like a queen.

♟ → Q
03

Protect the king

Kings are still kings. Checkmate wins the game. Castling is only available while the king and rook still move like themselves.

♚ + ♜ = castle
