PILOT GUIDE

How to fly.

DRAG Steer

Drag anywhere in the lower left to bank and climb. The ship self-levels when you let go.

TILT Optional motion

Enable the accelerometer on the title screen. On iPhone and iPad, iOS asks for motion permission — allow it to fly hands-free.

AUTO Cannons fire themselves

Line up a bandit inside the reticle. Twin cannons auto-engage with a little aim assist.

MISSILE Missile volley

Three charges. Missiles lock the nearest target and hit hard. Recharges over time.

ROLL Barrel roll

A short invulnerable dodge. Roll through plasma and turret fire. Brief cooldown.

BOOST Afterburner

Hold to burn forward and recharge. Releases refill the meter. Speed is survival.
