Field notes

How to play

Move with WASD or arrow keys. On touchscreens, use the directional pad.

Drop a bomb with Space or the BOMB button. Blasts travel in four directions and break cracked walls. Keep clear!

Strike with Z or SWORD when a creature gets too close. Find the Sun Key, unlock the vault, and reach the glowing exit.
